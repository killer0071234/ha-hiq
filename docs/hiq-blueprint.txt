LC-10-IQ
--------------------
*** on/off lights
entity: switch
quantity: 80 (8 devices, 10 entities each)
autodetect: lc00_general_error..lc07_general_error (0-connected, 1-disconnected)
read/write: lc00_qx00..lc07_qx09 (0-off, 1-on)

*** button inputs
entity: button
quantity: 80 (8 devices, 10 entities each)
autodetect: lc00_general_error..lc07_general_error (0-connected, 1-disconnected)
read by event, check socket specifications (0-short press, 1-short release, 2-long press, 3-long release)

configuration parameters:

*** input mode, defines how input affects the output: 0-toggle on/off, 1-timer extend edge (staircase), 2-timer extend level (motion sensor), 3-timer extend level night only, 4-inverse (door sensor), 5-inverse night only, 6-direct (doorbell), 7-event only
read: lc00_input_mode_00..lc07_input_mode_09 (0..7)
write: lc00_input_mode_index (0..9), lc00_input_mode_data (0..7), lc00_input_mode_req=1

*** output timer, timer that automatically turns off the output: 0-no timer, 1..9999-auto off (seconds)
read: lc00_output_mode_00..lc07_output_mode_09 (0..9999)
write: lc00_output_mode_index (0..9), lc00_output_mode_data (0..9999), lc00_output_mode_req=1


LD-10-IQ
--------------------
*** dimmable lights
entity: light
quantity: 40 (4 devices, 10 entities each)
autodetect: ld00_general_error..ld03_general_error (0-connected, 1-disconnected)
read/write: ld00_qx00 (0-off, 1-on), ld00_qw00 (0..100)

*** button inputs
entity: button
quantity: 40 (4 devices, 10 entities each)
autodetect: ld00_general_error..ld03_general_error (0-connected, 1-disconnected)
read by event, check socket specifications (0-short press, 1-short release, 2-long press, 3-long release)

configuration parameters:

*** input mode, defines how input affects the output
read: ld00_input_mode_00..ld03_input_mode_09 (0..7)
write: ld00_config_group=0, ld00_config_index (0..9), ld00_config_data (0..7), ld00_config_req=1

*** output timer, timer that automatically turns off the output
read: ld00_output_timer_00..ld03_output_timer_09 (0..9999)
write: ld00_config_group=1, ld00_config_index (0..9), ld00_config_data (0..9999), ld00_config_req=1

*** color mode: 0-standard dimmer, 1-RGB dimmer, 2-white temperature
read: ld00_color_mode_00..ld03_color_mode_09 (0..2)
write: ld00_config_group=2, ld00_config_index (0..9), ld00_config_data (0..2), ld00_config_req=1

TH-1-IQ
TH-2-IQ
--------------------
entity: climate
quantity: 6 (6 devices)
autodetect: th00_general_error..th05_general_error (0-connected, 1-disconnected)

*** measured values
read: th00_temperature [0.1°C]
read: th00_humidity [%]

*** thermostat active
read/write: th00_active (0-off, 1-on)

*** setpoint
read: th00_setpoint_active [0.1°C] - current setpoint
read/write: th00_setpoint [0.1°C] - primary setpoint

configuration parameters:

*** source of measured temperature (0-internal sensor, 1-external sensor, 2-remote value)
read: th00_temperature_source
write: th00_temperature_source (0..2), th00_config1_req=1

*** measurement correction
read: th00_temperature_offset [0.1°C]
write: th00_temperature_offset [0.1°C], th00_config1_req=1

*** setpoint limits
read: th00_setpoint_lo, th00_setpoint_hi [*0.1°C]
write: th00_setpoint_lo, th00_setpoint_hi [*0.1°C], th00_config2_req=1

*** secondary setpoint, used when thermostat is off
read: th00_setpoint_idle [*0.1°C]
write: th00_setpoint_idle [*0.1°C], th00_config2_req=1

*** window switch enable
read: th00_window_enable
write: th00_window_enable, th00_config3_req=1
